## Retrieval and transfer

Core reading fronts withhold pronunciation; core listening fronts withhold the form. Word and checkpoint meanings are answer-side context, closing the earlier exact-answer leaks from sushi/memo/tomato. Actual checkpoint fronts remain Japanese-only with no romaji, audio or translation hint.

The comparisons are explicitly identification among visible alternatives. They are useful targeted discrimination practice but not evidence of unaided recall. Separate core listening questions supply that uncued direction. Variant items extend recognition beyond the initial typeface without assessing handwriting.

The final 14 targets remain new words relative to prior teaching examples, including the newly added guide/reference material. あさ occurs only as a substring in earlier おばあさん/おかあさん, not as the previously taught word “morning”. Unseen status is course-relative on first encounter; later scheduled reviews are practice, not fresh unseen tests.

The selected reading applications and held-out examples give useful transfer beyond single glyphs, chart positions and memorized teaching words. Success on this finite set still does not certify all unfamiliar-word reading. Accurate unaided decoding with appropriate length and doubled consonants is the stated criterion; no speed threshold is added.

## Pacing, scope and claims

At six new scheduled questions/day, 290 questions require at least 49 introduction days. Reviews, pauses and individual pacing extend that minimum. No fixed deadline or mastery promise follows from this count, and ten-minute feasibility has not yet been measured in a realistic learner session.

The course keeps meanings as context, uses isolated kana/words, and avoids particle-use exercises, grammar, kanji-learning tasks, compulsory handwriting and systematic pitch-accent training. Some provider inputs contain kanji for lexical disambiguation; learner prompts do not.

All 131 referenced sound files in the reviewed inventory have declared ja-JP-NanamiNeural and −20% provenance. That confirms declared configuration only. This reviewer has not listened to the audio and makes no pronunciation, segmentation or native-listener-quality approval.

The source's studyRecommendations correctly identifies #805 as a separate app dependency. Manual prototype instructions are not a substitute for the brief's supported automatic introduction-order/defaults launch requirement.
