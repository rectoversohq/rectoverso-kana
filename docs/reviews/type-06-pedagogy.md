## Objective, visible forms and feedback

Each question asks the learner to read an already introduced kana in a different style. The single image supplies the visual form, while its sound and standard-character identification are withheld until reveal. This tests recognition beyond the original course typeface and chart position without adding a new sound.

All eight pictured glyphs are recognizable as their declared targets. The Yomogi images offer visible changes in connection/separation, especially さ and き; the serif examples alter stroke endings and weight while preserving character identity. The supplied images contain no romaji or target label.

The emitted image alt text is generic in both languages; it does not disclose the target kana. There is no question audio. The answer contains the reading and its existing sound recording; the Note supplies the standard kana and explains the style. Target kana, reading and audio agree with the basic inventory for all eight records.

The English/French notes accurately frame Yomogi as a handwritten-style typeface, not a stroke-order demonstration. They explicitly say that copying the style is unnecessary. The task therefore remains optional-handwriting-compatible recognition practice, not handwriting production or assessment.

The examples establish exposure to two additional typefaces, not comprehensive ability to read arbitrary personal handwriting. The course copy does not overclaim that coverage.

## Progression and workload

All eight targets follow their basic reading introductions. In the reviewed 272-question sequence, hiragana variants appear at positions 138–142 and katakana variants at 270–272, after the corresponding basic/rule blocks. Existing listening reverses retain at least one intervening question after their reading prerequisites.

The later placement preserves an initially consistent visual teaching style before adding variation. No separate deck or scheduled question for every font/character is required. Eight targeted additions are bounded and fit the brief's selected-variant approach.

These questions use the same six-new-question/day allowance. The current 272-question inventory requires at least 46 introduction days at that cap, before pauses and any later additions; this is not a promised completion duration or a measured review workload.

## Build evidence

Both revision-2 Anki verification reports state eight cards and passes for import, front/back, media, real study and reimport preservation. The reports were inspected, not rerun by this reviewer. Revision-2 per-image font-credit changes do not alter the inspected question pixels.

The audio references reuse basic recordings with declared Nanami −20% provenance. This reviewer did not listen to those recordings and makes no native-listener or auditory-quality claim.

## Remaining limits

- The PNGs are inspected artifacts, not native screenshots. Native image scaling, readability, cropping, dark-plate appearance in both app themes, full Note access and answer-audio access remain pending.
- Reusing the dark plate in both appearances is not itself a pedagogical defect; the native presentation must still make the glyph clearly visible.
- Only the selected typefaces/forms are covered. Later unaided word reading remains the stronger evidence of practical decoding transfer.
- The simulator was not driven or reacquired.
- Source order and due values do not implement the separate #805 app defaults or establish mastery gating.
